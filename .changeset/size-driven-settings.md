Settings that depend on a base's size, its height, top thickness, corner radius, label size and supports, now follow the size until you set them yourself, and picking a standard size no longer resets your bottom edge or other choices. Sizes are typed directly with standard sizes as chips beneath, the counter's click follows its wheel size, and batch moves to the end of the base settings. Every panel gains an Advanced settings switch that hides fine-tuning until you change it, and token and spray-tray settings put their first choices first. On a phone every generator fits across the screen, downloads read STL and 3MF, and the title block shrinks to a strip so the model keeps the screen.
