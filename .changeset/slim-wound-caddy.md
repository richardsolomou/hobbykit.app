Rebuild the dial counter around dials that click on spring arms, in a choice of layouts: an enclosure with a window over the count and knobs at the sides, open hubs with knobs, hubs on legs, or a frame on a base. Tiles and the lines framing each digit run each dial's full width, every body prints in one colour, and the top-row brackets print with the tiles. Saved counters move to the new defaults.
