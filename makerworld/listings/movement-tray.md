# Magnetised movement trays

- **Script:** `movement-tray.scad`
- **Category:** Toys & Games › Board Games
- **Tags:** movement tray, regiment tray, the old world, kings of war, warhammer, rank and flank, magnetised, wargaming, customizable, parametric, no supports

## Summary

Movement trays for any regiment, square or round bases, with magnet pockets under every slot. Set the frontage and ranks.

## Description

Choose the base size, how many bases go across the front and how many ranks deep. Rectangular bases share one recess so the ranks stay in contact. Round bases each get their own slot. Pockets under every slot sit in the same places as the magnets in HobbyKit's magnetised bases, so the unit holds onto the tray when you pick it up.

What you can change:

- Unit: any standard round or rectangular size or a custom one, with up to 10 bases across the front and 10 ranks deep
- Slots: depth and clearance
- Construction: the floor thickness and the rim around the recess
- Magnets: on or off, size, count per base, and the pocket layout
- Fit: magnet clearances and the base settings that decide where their magnets sit

## Print settings

- Prints flat on its table face with the slots facing up
- Supports off
- 0.2mm layers
- PLA or PETG

## Bill of materials

- Optional: neodymium magnets, 5×2mm by default. Leave them out for a steel-paint or plain tray.
